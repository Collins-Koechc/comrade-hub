Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

At ComradeHub, accessible from our platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by ComradeHub and how we use it.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our platform and other sites on the internet.

Information We Collect & Upload Infrastructure

ComradeHub is a public document repository. Files uploaded to this website (including PDF documents, images, and spreadsheets) are stored on public servers and can be downloaded by any visitor. Do not upload personal, confidential, or private academic records.

Log Files

ComradeHub follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages.
