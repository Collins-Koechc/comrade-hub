About Our Platform

Welcome to ComradeHub, a shared open-access repository built to bridge the gap in academic materials access across student networks.

Our Shared Philosophy

"Give what you have, take what you need!"

Finding high-quality past revision papers, clean module notes, or study summaries shouldn't be an expensive obstacle. ComradeHub allows campus communities to collect files in a structured environment—making tracking material simple via course codes and academic institutional metadata tags.

Platform Governance

The matrix is constantly maintained and reviewed for clean user execution. We welcome collaborative institutional suggestions to grow our indexed categories safely and protect educational value.
